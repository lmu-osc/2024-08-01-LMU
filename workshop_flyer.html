About the Workshop

The workshop will cover the Unix shell, version control in git, and programming in Python. It is free and open to researchers, staff, and students. This workshop teaches core computing skills that will help you be more productive and effective. We will alternate short tutorials with hands-on practical exercises. Participants will be encouraged to help one another and to apply what they have learned to their own research problems.

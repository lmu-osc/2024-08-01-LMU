About the Workshop

Topics: Unix Shell, Version Control with Git, Programming in Python

The two-day software carpentry workshop is free and open to researchers, staff, and students. It teaches core computing skills that will help you be more productive and effective. We will alternate short tutorials with hands-on practical exercises. Participants will be encouraged to help one another and to apply what they have learned to their own research problems.
